How the wind works

  1. 1

    Plant a meadow

    Your browser gets a secret room name from a tiny public “matchmaker” server (PeerJS). That is all the server ever sees.

  2. 2

    Share the link

    Your friend opens the link. The two browsers shake hands with WebRTC and open a direct data channel.

  3. 3

    Let it drift

    Messages fly browser to browser. Tabs in the same browser also talk through BroadcastChannel, even offline.
